There have been several specific and practical suggestions, but they were spread over several messages in the discussion. I'll summarize here:

1. Use random IVs instead of counter or state-derived IVs.

2. For any crypto scheme that uses random numbers or IVs, generate the random numbers/IVs after the message to be encrypted and/or authenticated is fixed.

3. Use the operating system's secure RNG to generate these random numbers/IVs and hash in the current time and/or the message to make sure random numbers are not reused on different messages.

4. As an alternative to 1-3 above, switch to a crypto scheme such as SIV that is specifically designed to tolerate nonce reuse.
