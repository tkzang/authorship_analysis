Oh, right. AIXI-tl works a bit differently in this regard from Schmidhuber's Godel Machine, and what I wrote only applies to the Godel Machine. But the situation here isn't any better. In the case of AIXI-tl, a policy isn't allowed to make use of unproved conjectures in estimating its future value, because then it would be unable to prove that the estimated value is not more than the xi-expected value. So a policy that implicitly or explicitly assumes a conjecture that is likely to be true has no advantage over a policy that assumes the opposite. Instead of the assumed conjectures being fixed as in Godel Machine's case, in an AIXI-tl they would instead change randomly from policy to policy.
